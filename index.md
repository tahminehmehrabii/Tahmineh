My research combines wet-lab experimentation and computational analysis to investigate tumor heterogeneity, cancer-associated molecular signatures, and biomarkers. I integrate single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, bulk RNA-seq, and machine learning with experimental approaches to study the tumor microenvironment, cell–cell communication, and interactions among cancer cells, immune populations, and the microbiome. My goal is to advance our understanding of cancer biology and identify clinically relevant diagnostic and prognostic biomarkers.
