I am a **computational cancer researcher** with experience integrating **bulk RNA-seq, single-cell RNA-seq, and spatial transcriptomic data** to investigate tumor heterogeneity, the tumor microenvironment, spatial cell interactions, and clinically relevant biomarkers.

My research focuses on **spatial multi-omics, single-cell cancer genomics, precision oncology, prognostic biomarker discovery, survival modeling, machine learning, and external validation**. I work primarily with **R, Python, and Linux**, using computational approaches to connect molecular patterns with tumor biology and clinical outcomes.

My recent projects include multi-omics studies in **lung adenocarcinoma** and **laryngeal squamous cell carcinoma**, with additional experience in prostate, pancreatic, and breast cancer research. Alongside computational work, I have hands-on experience in **molecular biology and cancer cell culture**, which helps me approach research questions from both computational and experimental perspectives.

I hold an **M.Sc. in Cellular and Molecular Biology** from **Azad University, Tehran Medical Sciences Branch**, with a GPA of **3.82/4.00**.
